How it resolves

Humans resolve the market: an expert jury compares repositories pairwise, and the market's weights are checked against their judgement of original work.

Original work, over time

The live product charts each repository's share of original work over time — in PRISM, the light sweep across the field plays that role: a slow read of where the weight sits now.
