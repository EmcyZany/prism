02

Create, trade, resolve

How Seer works, from the live product.

Create Unique Markets

Bring your ideas to life! Design custom prediction markets on anything from sports outcomes to political events. Set your rules and engage with a vibrant community eager to join in. Seer allows you to create four different types of Market covering a wide range of possibilities.

Trade with Ease

Buy and sell shares with ease, monitor real-time data, and make informed decisions seamlessly. Seer employs the Conditional Tokens framework to create and manage outcome tokens efficiently. Enhancing usability, Seer wraps these ERC1155 outcome tokens into user-friendly ERC20 tokens, facilitating smoother trading and integration with existing DeFi protocols.

Secured by Kleros · Resolved by Reality.eth

Seer uses Kleros to resolve disputes that may arise when answering Reality.eth questions. It functions as a decentralized arbiter capable of determining the correct answer by applying a set of rules to questions ranging from simple to highly complex.
