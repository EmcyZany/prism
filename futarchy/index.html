Futarchy · Markets know better than experts

Two futures, flowing over each other.

Market-based evaluation of governance decisions and milestones. Every proposal carries two conditional prices — the metric if it passes and if it fails — and the gap between them is the headline. In PRISM the two futures are two liquid layers: slide the passes price and watch one future rise over the other.
