How a card reads

The anatomy of every market on Seer, kept from the live app.

Verified media

Each card carries its Kleros Curate status — "SVG Image Verified" — so the image attached to a market is the one the curators approved.

On-chain, inspectable

"View contract on explorer" and the network link (Gnosisscan on Gnosis) sit on the card, not buried in a detail page.

Conditional, labelled

Conditional markets state their condition on the card itself — "Conditional on … being Yes/No" — and some markets carry a reward, e.g. 300.00k SEER/day.
