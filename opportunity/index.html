Opportunity Markets

With AI, the cost of reviewing work is more than the time it takes to do it

Opportunity Markets turn any selection process into a market. Traders put money behind the submission they think will be selected, the odds move, and sponsors prioritize review on only top rated submissions.

·

The pilot — Seer AI Job Board Name

Markets live · ACTIVE · $2.39K liquidity · 235 outcomes · prices across the field sum to 100%

A rail, not a feed — nothing here scrolls on its own. The sponsor walks the field at their own pace.

The sponsor makes the final decision

The market is a triage tool — it ranks, it never decides. However the rail is ordered, the final call on the AI job board's name belongs to the sponsor, and traders earn P&L based on that decision.
